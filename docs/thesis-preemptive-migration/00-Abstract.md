摘  要  
面向物联网与时延敏感服务的边缘计算系统，常受资源受限、负载动态变化与故障频发等因素影响，易引发性能退化与服务中断。容器迁移可提升服务连续性，但在边缘环境中实现主动容错的抢占式迁移仍面临三方面关键挑战：其一，边缘节点运行状态具有显著时空相关性与不确定性，故障风险难以被稳定刻画；其二，迁移决策需要在能耗、时延（SLA）与迁移开销之间实现可控权衡；其三，缺乏有效约束时，频繁迁移易导致系统震荡与资源浪费。针对上述问题，本文围绕“面向主动容错的边缘计算抢占式迁移”开展研究，构建一套基于生成对抗学习的迁移方法体系，并给出从单目标到多目标优化的渐进式改进方案。

首先，本文提出基础模型 FPE-GAN：利用时空特征编码器提取故障风险信号，在异常检测触发条件下生成候选迁移调度，并结合仿真评估与保守筛选机制过滤低收益迁移，从而抑制不必要的迁移行为。其次，针对时序表征不稳定导致的决策波动问题，本文提出 TF-GAN，引入 Transformer 编码器增强时间依赖建模能力与可扩展性，提高迁移决策一致性。最后，为解决多指标目标冲突与约束难以显式表达的问题，本文提出 MAMO-GAN，通过迁移感知生成器与多目标判别器，将能耗、响应时间与迁移规模等约束显式融入对抗训练反馈，实现更可控的多指标权衡。为保证训练与评估口径一致，本文在联合仿真平台上设计三阶段流程，并采用统一的故障事件定义与基于 Actual-Detected-Expected（ADE）的标注机制完成数据生成与模型训练。

实验结果表明，在相同仿真环境与工作负载设置下，本文方法在迁移规模控制方面具有优势，迁移次数相较代表性启发式基线减少约 4%–63%。在所提方法内部对比中，TF-GAN 进一步提升迁移决策稳定性；MAMO-GAN 在能耗与迁移方差等方面表现更稳健，同时 SLA 违约率稳定在约 12%–13%。消融实验验证了 Transformer 编码器、基于 GAT 的空间建模、迁移感知机制与多目标判别器等关键组件在故障风险刻画、迁移稳定性与能耗优化方面的有效性。综上，本文为边缘环境下主动容错的抢占式迁移提供了一种可解释、可扩展且支持多目标约束的数据驱动解决方案。

关键词：边缘计算；抢占式迁移；主动容错；生成对抗网络；多目标优化



**Abstract**  
Edge computing systems deployed for IoT and latency-sensitive services often suffer from limited resources, highly dynamic workloads, and frequent failures, which can lead to performance degradation and service disruption. Container migration is an effective means of improving service continuity; however, proactive migration (also referred to as preemptive migration) is challenging due to the need for accurate failure-risk estimation and controllable trade-offs among energy consumption, latency/SLA, and migration overhead. Traditional heuristic approaches typically struggle to capture spatio-temporal dependencies and to maintain stable multi-metric optimization, leading to either insufficient protection or excessive migrations and oscillations.

This thesis investigates proactive fault-tolerant migration for edge computing and proposes a GAN-based methodological framework with progressive improvements from single-objective to multi-objective optimization. First, we develop FPE-GAN as a baseline approach: a spatio-temporal feature encoder captures failure-risk signals, and candidate schedules are generated only when anomalies are detected; a conservative screening mechanism, coupled with simulation-based evaluation, is used to filter out low-benefit migrations. Second, we propose TF-GAN by introducing a Transformer-based encoder to improve temporal representation and scalability, leading to more consistent decisions. Third, we propose MAMO-GAN to address multi-objective conflicts by integrating a migration-aware generator and a multi-objective discriminator, incorporating energy, response-time, and migration-scale constraints into the learning signal to enable more controllable trade-offs. To ensure consistent training and evaluation criteria, we adopt a three-stage workflow on a coupled simulation platform and apply a unified definition of failure events with an Actual-Detected-Expected (ADE)-based labeling mechanism.

Experimental results show that the proposed methods effectively control migration scale, achieving approximately 4%–63% fewer migrations than several representative heuristic baselines under the same simulation settings. Within the proposed family, TF-GAN further improves stability, while MAMO-GAN provides more robust performance in terms of energy consumption and migration variance, with SLA violation rates remaining stable at roughly 12%–13%. Ablation studies confirm the contributions of key components, including the Transformer encoder, GAT-based spatial modeling, migration awareness, and the multi-objective discriminator, to failure-risk estimation, migration stability, and energy optimization. Overall, this thesis provides an interpretable, extensible, data-driven solution with multi-objective constraints for proactive fault-tolerant migration in edge environments.

**Keywords:** edge computing, proactive migration, fault tolerance, generative adversarial networks, multi-objective optimization
