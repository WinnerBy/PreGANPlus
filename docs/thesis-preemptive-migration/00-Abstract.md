摘  要  
边缘计算面向物联网与实时服务场景时，常受到资源受限、负载波动与故障频发等因素影响，容易引发性能退化与服务中断。容器迁移能够提升服务连续性，但抢占式迁移在“故障风险表征—迁移决策生成—多指标约束权衡”上存在显著挑战：一方面，边缘节点状态具有时空相关性与不确定性，传统启发式方法难以稳定捕捉故障风险；另一方面，迁移决策需在能耗、时延（SLA）与迁移成本之间取得可控权衡，避免频繁迁移带来的系统震荡。为此，本文围绕“面向主动容错的边缘计算抢占式迁移”开展研究，提出一套基于生成对抗学习的抢占式迁移方法体系，并给出从单目标到多目标的渐进式改进方案。  
具体而言，本文首先提出基础模型 FPE-GAN：通过时空特征编码实现故障风险感知，并在异常触发下生成候选调度，结合仿真评估与保守筛选机制抑制无收益迁移。随后，本文提出 TF-GAN，引入 Transformer 编码器以提升时序表征的稳定性与可扩展性，增强迁移决策的一致性。进一步地，本文提出面向多目标冲突的 MAMO-GAN，通过迁移感知生成器与多目标判别器将能耗、响应时间与迁移规模等约束显式注入训练反馈，实现更可控的多指标权衡。为保证训练与评估口径一致，本文在联合仿真平台上采用三阶段流程，并基于统一的故障事件定义与 ADE 标签机制完成数据生成与模型训练。  
实验结果表明，在相同仿真环境与工作负载设置下，所提方法在迁移规模控制方面具有优势，相比部分传统方法迁移次数降幅约为 4%–63%；在方法内部对比中，TF-GAN 能进一步提升迁移决策稳定性，而 MAMO-GAN 在能耗与迁移方差等方面表现更稳健，SLA 违约率保持在约 12%–13% 的稳定区间。消融实验进一步验证了 Transformer、GAT、迁移感知机制与多目标判别器等关键组件对故障表征能力、迁移稳定性与能耗优化的贡献。综上，本文工作为边缘环境下主动容错的抢占式迁移提供了一种可解释、可扩展且具备多目标约束能力的数据驱动方案。

关键词：边缘计算；抢占式迁移；主动容错；生成对抗网络；多目标优化  


Abstract  
Edge computing systems deployed for IoT and latency-sensitive services often suffer from limited resources, highly dynamic workloads, and frequent failures, which may cause performance degradation and service disruption. Container migration is an effective means to improve service continuity; however, proactive (preemptive) migration is challenging due to the need for accurate failure-risk representation and controllable trade-offs among energy consumption, latency/SLA, and migration overhead. Traditional heuristic approaches typically struggle to model spatio-temporal dependencies and to maintain stable multi-metric optimization, leading to either insufficient protection or excessive migrations and oscillations.  
This thesis investigates proactive fault-tolerant preemptive migration for edge computing and proposes a GAN-based methodological framework with progressive improvements from single-objective to multi-objective optimization. First, we develop FPE-GAN as a baseline approach: a spatio-temporal feature encoder captures failure-risk signals, and candidate schedules are generated only when anomalies are triggered; a conservative screening mechanism with simulation-based evaluation is employed to suppress low-benefit migrations. Second, we propose TF-GAN by introducing a Transformer-based encoder to enhance temporal representation stability and scalability, thereby improving decision consistency. Third, we propose MAMO-GAN to address multi-objective conflicts by integrating a migration-aware generator and a multi-objective discriminator, explicitly injecting energy, response-time, and migration-scale constraints into training feedback for more controllable trade-offs. To ensure consistent training and evaluation criteria, we adopt a three-stage workflow on a coupled simulation platform and use a unified failure-event definition with an ADE-based labeling mechanism.  
Experimental results show that the proposed methods effectively control migration scale, achieving approximately 4%–63% fewer migrations than several representative heuristic baselines under the same simulation settings. Within the proposed family, TF-GAN further improves stability, while MAMO-GAN provides more robust performance in terms of energy and migration variance, with SLA violation rates remaining stable at roughly 12%–13%. Ablation studies confirm the contributions of key components, including the Transformer encoder, GAT-based spatial modeling, migration-awareness, and the multi-objective discriminator, to failure representation, migration stability, and energy optimization. Overall, this thesis provides an interpretable, extensible, and multi-objective constrained data-driven solution for proactive fault-tolerant preemptive migration in edge environments.

Key words: Edge Computing; Proactive Migration; Fault Tolerance; Generative Adversarial Networks; Multi-Objective Optimization
